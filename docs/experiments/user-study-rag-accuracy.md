# User Study: RAG Accuracy in Research Paper Q&A

## Experiment ID

`user-study-rag-accuracy`

## Hypothesis

RAG with 5 relevant documents achieves 80% factual accuracy in paper Q&A, dropping to 60% with 10 documents due to noise.

## Method

Recruit 20 researchers. Each asks 10 questions about ArXiv papers. Compare answers with/without RAG, varying document count.

## Variables

- **Document Count** (independent): Number of retrieved documents — 3, 5, 10, 15
- **Factual Accuracy** (dependent): Percentage of correct factual claims
- **User Satisfaction** (dependent): 1-5 rating of answer helpfulness

## Expected Results

Peak accuracy at 5 documents, degradation beyond 7 due to irrelevant context.
