## 摘要
当社会联系可以形成和瓦解时，稳定的个体人格差异如何塑造合作？我们在一个内生网络上的重复囚徒困境中建模，其中三个连续的大五人格特质映射为透明的局部机制：外向性设定目标伙伴数，开放性决定智能体在朋友的朋友之外搜索的广度，宜人性设定合作意愿的基线。在每次互动中，智能体将该基线与对伙伴的直接观察历史相结合；不存在特质标签、流言或全局声誉。当智能体连接不足时形成联系，当连接过多时切断联系，切断时优先剔除更常背叛的伙伴。我们改变网络规模（N=30–200）、种群组成，以及特质驱动行为与历史驱动行为之间的平衡。

三个稳健的模式浮现。首先，先合作、后互惠——较高的初始合作意愿与对历史的敏感响应相结合——所产生的系统在繁荣、公平和安全方面均更优。其次，人格具有可预测的条件效应：当历史重要时宜人性有益，但在行为主要由特质驱动时则有害；外向性放大了环境的影响；开放性的净收益效应很小。第三，网络据此重组：度同配性保持在零附近，而当合作确立时，宜人性较高的智能体之间越来越倾向于彼此连接。

## Abstract
How do stable personality differences shape cooperation when social ties can form and dissolve? We model a repeated Prisoner's Dilemma on an endogenous network in which three continuous Big Five traits map to transparent local mechanisms: Extraversion sets a target number of partners, Openness determines how broadly agents search beyond friends-of-friends, and Agreeableness sets a baseline willingness to cooperate. At each encounter, agents combine this baseline with the partner's directly observed history; there are no trait labels, gossip, or global reputations. Ties form when agents are under-connected and are cut when they become over-connected, with cuts prioritising partners who have defected more often. We vary network size (N=30--200), population composition, and the balance between trait-driven and history-driven behaviour.   Three robust patterns emerge. First, cooperate first, then reciprocate---high initial willingness to cooperate combined with history-sensitive response---produces systems that are simultaneously more prosperous, fairer, and safer. Second, personality has predictable conditional effects: Agreeableness helps when history matters but hurts when behaviour is mostly trait-driven; Extraversion amplifies the environment; Openness has little net payoff effect. Third, the network reorganises accordingly: degree assortativity stays near zero, whereas agreeable agents increasingly connect to one another when cooperation takes hold.
