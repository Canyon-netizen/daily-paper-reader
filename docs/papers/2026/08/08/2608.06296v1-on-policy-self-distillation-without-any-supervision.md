## Abstract
On-policy (Self-)Distillation (OPD / OPSD) has shown strong potential for post-training large language models (LLMs). However, existing methods still rely heavily on external supervision, including ground-truth signals, environmental feedback, or guidance from larger models, and therefore fall short of genuine "self"-distillation. In this study, we show that on-policy self-distillation can be achieved using only a model's own generations via internal consistency. We propose Unsupervised On-Policy Self-Distillation (U-OPSD). U-OPSD first samples multiple rollouts and constructs a pseudo-solution by majority vote under a self-consistency threshold. It then conditions a teacher distribution on the shortest pseudo-solution and distills it into prefixes of the model's longest incorrect completion, allowing the model to correct itself precisely where it is confidently wrong. Across diverse benchmarks, base models, and training settings, U-OPSD consistently improves over the base models and matches or surpasses supervised methods with ground truth (GT), such as OPSD and GRPO. On AIME24, AIME25, HMMT25, MATH500, and AMC23, U-OPSD improves over the base model by 8.5% and 10.7% on Qwen3 non-thinking mode at the 4B and 8B scales, respectively, and outperforms OPSD by an average of 3.2% and 2.3%. In thinking mode, U-OPSD remains on par with OPSD, outperforming it by 0.9% at 4B and matching it at 8B, while surpassing GRPO by 0.7% and 1.1%, respectively.

## 1. 核心问题与研究动机

- **研究背景**：在线策略（自）蒸馏（OPD / OPSD）已成为 LLM 后训练的重要范式，介于 SFT 与 RL 之间，能保留稠密的 token 级监督，同时减少训练-推理不匹配与灾难性遗忘。
- **现有痛点**：现有方法仍依赖**外部监督**——真实标签（GT）、环境反馈、或更强的教师模型。所谓"自蒸馏"仅是师生共享参数，但信息源仍来自模型外部。
- **核心问题**：能否在**完全无外部监督**的条件下，让模型通过自身的内部一致性完成真正的自蒸馏？论文将其概括为：在线策略自蒸馏中的教师是否真正需要真实解？
- **核心洞察**：虽然模型单次采样的 rollout 不稳定，但多次独立 rollout 的**多数共识**可作为内生的置信信号，从中可同时构造教师参考与学生轨迹。

## 2. 方法论

### 2.1 核心思想

对每一个无标签问题 $x \sim \mathcal{U}$，采样 $G$ 次独立 rollouts：

$$y^{(1)}, \ldots, y^{(G)} \stackrel{\text{i.i.d.}}{\sim} \bar{\pi}(\cdot \mid x), \quad \bar{\pi} \triangleq \pi_{\text{sg}[\theta]}$$

提取答案 $a^{(g)} = \text{Ans}(y^{(g)})$，做多数投票得到伪答案 $\tilde{a}(x)$，并据此将 rollouts 划分为**一致集** $Y_x^+$ 与**不一致集** $Y_x^-$。将一致集内最短的 rollout $y^+$ 作为伪解（教师参考），在不一致集 $Y_x^-$ 的前缀上做蒸馏。

### 2.2 整体目标函数

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{U-OPSD}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{x \sim \mathcal{U}}\, \mathbb{E}_{\{y^{(g)}\} \sim \bar{\pi}(\cdot|x)} \left[ \mathbb{1}[Y_x^- \neq \emptyset] \cdot \frac{1}{|Y_x^-|} \sum_{y^- \in Y_x^-} \frac{1}{|y^-|} \sum_{n=1}^{|y^-|} D_\beta\!\left( \bar{\pi}(\cdot \mid x, y^+, y^-_{<n}) \;\|\; \pi_\theta(\cdot \mid x, y^-_{<n}) \right) \right]$$

其中 $D_\beta$ 为广义的 Jensen–Shannon 散度，$\beta \to 0$ 时退化为前向 KL 散度 $D_{\text{KL}}(P \| Q)$。

### 2.3 算法流程

1. **Sample**：对每个 prompt $x$ 采样 $G$ 个 rollout，提取答案 $a^{(g)} = \text{Ans}(y^{(g)})$，无效回答标记为 $\emptyset$。
2. **Vote**：在有效回答中用多数投票得出 $\tilde{a}(x)$，计算自一致性分数 $c(x) = \frac{1}{G}\sum_g \mathbb{1}[a^{(g)} = \tilde{a}(x)]$，阈值 $\tau = 1/2$。
3. **Distill**：若 $c(x) \geq \tau$ 且 $Y_x^- \neq \emptyset$，则以 $y^+ \in \arg\min_{y \in Y_x^+} |y|$ 为教师参考，在不一致样本的前缀上做 token 级蒸馏。

跳过两类样本：投票分数不足（共识不可靠）以及所有有效 rollouts 均一致（无错误可改），从而自然聚焦于模型的"能力边界"。

### 2.4 关键设计选择

- **教师冻结**：教师 $\bar{\pi}$ 固定为初始策略（与 OPSD 一致），便于公平对比。
- **目标函数**：前向 KL（$\beta = 0$）+ per-token 截断。
- **教师参考**：选**最长**一致 rollouts（保留完整推理链）。
- **蒸馏目标**：选**最长**不一致 rollouts（最具信息量的纠正目标）。
- **rollout 数**：默认 $G = 8$，阈值 $\tau = 0.5$。
