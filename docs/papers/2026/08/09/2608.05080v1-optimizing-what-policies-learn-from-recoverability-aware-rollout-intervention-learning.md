## Abstract
Critic-free group-based reinforcement learning has become a scalable approach for post-training large language models. However, most existing methods allocate the same number of rollouts to every task and trajectory state, even though some rollouts provide much more useful learning signals than others. Recent work has started to treat rollout generation as an adaptive decision, but two important limitations remain. First, intervention strategies are often based on fixed heuristics and therefore cannot adjust as the policy changes during training. Second, these methods usually decide only how many rollouts to generate, without explicitly controlling where and how to intervene. To address these limitations, we propose Recoverability-Aware Intervention Learning (RAIL), a training-time framework that learns how to generate rollouts based on the improvement produced by each intervention. RAIL models intervention selection as an online contextual-bandit problem and trains a recoverability controller using intervention traces collected through a shadow-to-live procedure. This allows the controller to keep learning while the underlying policy evolves. We evaluate RAIL in terms of effectiveness, adaptivity, expressiveness, and efficiency. Across multiple settings, RAIL consistently improves performance under limited rollout budgets. These results show that recoverability-aware intervention provides a principled way to generate more informative and less redundant rollouts, leading to stronger learning signals during post-training.

## 2. 方法论

### 2.1 核心概念：可恢复性（Recoverability）

论文提出 **可恢复性（recoverability）**——即在状态 $z$ 上施加干预 $b$ 后，rollout 分组的奖励对比度增益：

$$\Delta_\theta(z, b) = \mathbb{E}\left[I(Y_b) - I(Y_\emptyset) \mid z, b, \pi_\theta\right]$$

其中 $I(Y) = \frac{1}{|Y|}\sum_{y_i \in Y}(R(y_i) - \bar{R}_Y)^2$ 衡量有限组的奖励对比度。这一点具有理论支撑：在二元验证奖励下，奖励方差 $p(1-p)$ 在 $p=0.5$ 时最大，且与 group-based RL 的相对优势方差直接相关（论文 C.1 节给出证明）。

### 2.2 上下文赌博机建模

RAIL 将干预选择建模为**在线上下文赌博机（contextual bandit）**问题：
- **上下文（context）**：当前轨迹状态 $z_t$
- **动作（action）**：结构化干预 $b_t \in \mathcal{B}$
- **效用（reward）**：干预施加后**实际观测**到的可恢复性增益 $y_t$

由于未执行干预的反事实增益无法观测，控制器只能从**部分反馈**中学习。

### 2.3 可恢复性控制器（Recoverability Controller）

控制器是一个小型打分网络 $\hat{\Delta}_\phi(z, b)$，通过窗口化 Huber 回归拟合已观测干预轨迹：

$$\mathcal{L}_t(\phi) = \sum_{j \in I_t} w_{t-j}\,\ell_\kappa\!\left(\hat{\Delta}_\phi(z_j, b_j) - y_j\right)$$

其中 $I_t = \{j : \max(1, t-W+1) \le j \le t\}$，$w_{t-j}$ 是随时间衰减的最近权重。**关键设计**：使用 Huber 损失应对有限组奖励的噪声；窗口/最近权重使控制器能跟踪策略演化带来的可恢复性分布漂移。

### 2.4 结构化干预空间

干预空间显式联合控制**两个维度**：

$$\mathcal{B} = \mathcal{M} \times \mathcal{T}$$

- 分支预算 $\mathcal{M} = \{4, 8, 12\}$（额外生成的延续数）
- 解码策略 $\mathcal{T} = \{\text{EXPLOIT}, \text{MILD}, \text{AGGRESSIVE}\}$（温度/top-p 组合）

由于仅采用 $3 \times 3$ 共 9 个可执行干预格点，干预粒度足够精细（联合决策）但又足够小以便在线部分反馈学习。

### 2.5 Shadow-to-Live 部署流程

为避免冷启动时的不可靠预测，RAIL 采用两阶段部署：

**Shadow 阶段**：
- 使用基于高百分位决策熵的**启发式锚点检测**选择候选干预位置
- 在每个锚点按**预算递增顺序**（如 4→8→12）试探分支，若新增延续未提高奖励方差则停止（accept-or-stop）
- 每个被接受或拒绝的尝试都成为独立监督信号写入轨迹缓冲池 $(\mathbf{z}_t, b_t, y_t)$
- 控制器在缓冲池上用最近加权回归在线训练
- 提供**动态晋升规则**：连续 $P$ 次预序符号一致率 $\bar{a}_t$ 超过机会调整阈值 $\bar{\beta} = 0.5 + 2\sqrt{0.25/W}$ 后切换到 Live 模式；设置最大步数 $T_{\text{cap}}$ 安全兜底

**Live 阶段**：
- 控制器贪心选择 $\hat{\Delta}_\phi$ 最大的干预 $b_t^\star$
- **效用门控（utility gate）**：仅当 $\hat{\Delta}_\phi(z_t, b_t^\star) > \eta$ 时执行干预，否则维持默认 rollout
- 以概率 $\rho_t$ 进行**覆盖导向探索**（倾向于选择最少执行过的动作格），确保每个干预单元都获得非零覆盖
- 继续从新轨迹中**在线更新**控制器

### 2.6 理论结果

**定理（可恢复性遗憾）**：在有限干预空间 $\mathcal{B}$、有界奖励、充分探索、有限累积漂移 $D_T$ 与有限累积近似误差 $A_T$ 的条件下：

$$\text{Reg}_T \leq \tilde{O}\!\left(\sqrt{T|\mathcal{B}|}\right) + D_T + A_T$$

因此当 $D_T + A_T = o(T)$ 时，平均遗憾趋于 0；而固定启发式在代理与真实增益持续失配时则累积线性遗憾。

**理论还揭示**：固定熵信号与实际可恢复性增益的相关性极弱（Pearson $r = +0.01$），而学习到的控制器预测与实际增益相关性 $r = +0.31$，证明"可恢复性无法从熵读出，但可以学到"。
