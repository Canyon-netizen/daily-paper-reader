## 摘要

## Abstract
On-policy (Self-)Distillation (OPD / OPSD) has shown strong potential for post-training large language models (LLMs). However, existing methods still rely heavily on external supervision, including ground-truth signals, environmental feedback, or guidance from larger models, and therefore fall short of genuine "self"-distillation. In this study, we show that on-policy self-distillation can be achieved using only a model's own generations via internal consistency. We propose Unsupervised On-Policy Self-Distillation (U-OPSD). U-OPSD first samples multiple rollouts and constructs a pseudo-solution by majority vote under a self-consistency threshold. It then conditions a teacher distribution on the shortest pseudo-solution and distills it into prefixes of the model's longest incorrect completion, allowing the model to correct itself precisely where it is confidently wrong. Across diverse benchmarks, base models, and training settings, U-OPSD consistently improves over the base models and matches or surpasses supervised methods with ground truth (GT), such as OPSD and GRPO. On AIME24, AIME25, HMMT25, MATH500, and AMC23, U-OPSD improves over the base model by 8.5% and 10.7% on Qwen3 non-thinking mode at the 4B and 8B scales, respectively, and outperforms OPSD by an average of 3.2% and 2.3%. In thinking mode, U-OPSD remains on par with OPSD, outperforming it by 0.9% at 4B and matching it at 8B, while surpassing GRPO by 0.7% and 1.1%, respectively.

## 2. 方法论

### 2.1 核心思想
**关键洞察**：尽管单次 rollout 不可靠，但多次独立采样的"一致性"（agreement）可作为内生置信信号。基于此：
- **伪解构造**：用多数投票从自身采样中产生伪答案
- **教师参考**：用"达成共识的最短 rollout"作为教师的特权上下文
- **学生轨迹**：用"与共识不一致的 rollout"作为学生轨迹
- **精确纠错**：将教师分布蒸馏到学生轨迹的前缀上，模型在"自信犯错"处获得修正

### 2.2 符号与基本框架

设 $\pi_\theta$ 为 LLM 策略，$\bar{\pi} \triangleq \pi_{\text{sg}[\theta]}$ 为停止梯度副本（无梯度流过）。$y_{<t}$ 表示序列 $y$ 的前缀。$D_\beta(\cdot \| \cdot)$ 为广义 Jensen-Shannon 散度，其 $\beta \to 0$ 极限为 forward KL 散度。

**GRPO（基线对比）**：
$$L_{\text{GRPO}}(\theta) = -\mathbb{E}_{x \sim \mathcal{S}}\,\mathbb{E}_{\{y^{(g)}\}_{g=1}^{G} \sim \bar{\pi}(\cdot|x)} \frac{1}{G}\sum_{g=1}^{G}\frac{1}{|y^{(g)}|}\sum_{t=1}^{|y^{(g)}|} \min\Bigl(\rho_t^{(g)} A^{(g)},\, \text{clip}(\rho_t^{(g)}, 1{-}\varepsilon, 1{+}\varepsilon)A^{(g)}\Bigr)$$

其中 $A^{(g)}$ 为组内归一化优势，监督稀疏（序列级）。

**OPSD（监督版）**：
$$L_{\text{OPSD}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{(x,y^\star)\sim\mathcal{S}}\,\mathbb{E}_{y\sim\bar{\pi}(\cdot|x)} \sum_{t=1}^{|y|} D_\beta\Bigl(\pi_T(\cdot|x,y^\star,y_{<t}) \,\|\, \pi_S(\cdot|x,y_{<t})\Bigr)$$

其中 $\pi_S(\cdot|x) \triangleq \pi_\theta(\cdot|x)$，$\pi_T(\cdot|x,y^\star) \triangleq \bar{\pi}(\cdot|x,y^\star)$。

### 2.3 U-OPSD 完整算法

**步骤一（采样 Sample）**：对每个 prompt $x \sim \mathcal{U}$，采样 $G$ 次独立 rollout：
$$y^{(1)}, \dots, y^{(G)} \overset{\text{i.i.d.}}{\sim} \bar{\pi}(\cdot|x)$$
提取最终答案 $a^{(g)} = \text{Ans}(y^{(g)}) \in \mathcal{A} \cup \{\emptyset\}$。

**步骤二（投票 Vote）**：定义伪答案为多数投票：
$$\tilde{a}(x) = \underset{a}{\arg\max}\sum_{g=1}^{G}\mathbf{1}\bigl[a^{(g)} = a\bigr]$$

将 rollouts 划分为：
- 共识集：$\mathcal{Y}_x^+ = \{y^{(g)}: a^{(g)} = \tilde{a}(x)\}$
- 异议集：$\mathcal{Y}_x^- = \{y^{(g)}: a^{(g)} \neq \tilde{a}(x)\}$
- 无效（$\emptyset$）不属于两者

自一致性分数：
$$c(x) = \frac{1}{G}\sum_{g=1}^{G}\mathbf{1}\bigl[a^{(g)} = \tilde{a}(x)\bigr]$$

若 $c(x) < \tau$（默认 $\tau = 1/2$），prompt 不贡献梯度。

**步骤三（蒸馏 Distill）**：选取 $\mathcal{Y}_x^+$ 中最短的 $y^+$ 作为教师参考，对 $\mathcal{Y}_x^-$ 中的异议 rollouts 做 token 级蒸馏：

$$L_{\text{U-OPSD}}(\theta) = \sum_{i \in \mathcal{W}_k}\sum_{t=1}^{|y^{(i)}|} D_\beta\Bigl(\bar{\pi}(\cdot|x,y^+,y^{(i)}_{<t}) \,\|\, \pi_\theta(\cdot|x,y^{(i)}_{<t})\Bigr)$$

### 2.4 关键设计选择

| 组件 | 选择 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| 教师参考选择 | 最长共识 rollout | 完整推理链优于仅 boxed 答案 |
| 学生轨迹选择 | 最长异议 rollout | 覆盖更多需纠错位置 |
| 蒸馏目标数量 | $k=1$（disagree-1） | 最稳定、最具成本效益 |
| 散度 | Forward KL（$\beta=0$） | 反向 KL 致生成崩塌，JSD 退至基线 |
| 散度计算 | 全词表 logit 蒸馏 | 比 sampled-token 目标高 13.7 点 |
| 阈值 $\tau$ | 默认 $0.5$；$\tau=0$ 更佳 | 过滤的代价高于去除的噪声 |

### 8.3 方法局限
- **多数投票的隐含偏置**：倾向于较短的、较常见的答案，可能遗漏更长但正确的推理路径
- **未处理低质量共识**：当前缺乏投票质量自动检测/降权机制（作者将"投票边际"作为未来工作）
- **散度选择敏感**：反向 KL 导致生成崩塌（重复字符直至 token 上限），方法需谨慎选择散度

### 8.4 实验稳健性
- **缺乏完整 seed 复现误差棒**：仅靠多次评测减小方差，未做完整的种子重复
- **训练预算短**：150 步下结论，思考模式 rollout 极长，token 预算限制了训练时长
- **未测试对抗/扰动**：未在受污染的投票下验证鲁棒性

### 8.5 应用推广风险
- 对**无确定答案**的任务（开放问答、创意写作、对话）需设计"软共识"代替硬多数投票
- 对**非 Qwen3 基础模型**（如弱推理模型）效果未经验证——若一致性本身就很差，方法可能失效
- **推理时开销**：$G=8$ 次 rollout 大幅增加推理算力需求，但通过 vLLM 等效推理系统可缓解
