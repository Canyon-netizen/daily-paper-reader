## 摘要
学习与技能精通需要大量且刻意的练习。在许多学习场景中，产出高质量的教学材料往往需要高水平的领域专业知识，并且非常耗时。教学材料通常需要训练学生运用不同的思维模式。在某些领域（例如国际象棋）中，谜题被用来帮助学生练习计算后续着法和识别棋盘上已知模式的能力。为学生提供一套练习谜题以帮助他们学习不同的思维方式具有挑战性，因为教师需要在不同的主题以及学生所需进行的前瞻步数之间仔细平衡。Chess.com 和 Lichess 等流行的在线平台为玩家提供了数百万个谜题。与由人类专家精心编制的国际象棋战术谜题（初学者可以从中学习到宝贵的见解）不同，这些平台的谜题是自动生成的，通常被认为教学价值较低。这些平台还依赖启发式方法来向用户推荐练习谜题。我们使用覆盖一整年的用户历史数据，共计 15 亿条谜题解答记录，借助离线强化学习的见解，学习了谜题的教学价值，以及如何自动挑选谜题集合以更好地支持国际象棋学习者。我们表明，通过离线策略评估，我们训练得到的策略对谜题解答 Elo 范围在 100--1000 的初学者具有显著影响，尤其是对那些学习成长陷入停滞的初学者群体。我们还通过对国际象棋专家收集标注评分，对模型发现的谜题进行了定性分析。我们流程的成功表明，在未来，我们有望基于一般的用户交互数据来理解练习项目的教学价值。

## Abstract
Learning and skill mastery require extensive and deliberate practice. In many learning settings, producing high-quality pedagogical materials can require a high level of domain expertise and be very time-consuming. Pedagogical materials often need to train students to engage in different thinking patterns. In some domains, such as chess, puzzles are used to help students practice their skills in calculating the next moves and recognizing known patterns on a board. Giving students a practice set of puzzles to help them learn different modes of thinking is challenging because the teacher needs to carefully balance between different motifs and how many look-ahead steps a student needs to perform. Popular online platforms like Chess.com and Lichess offer players millions of puzzles. Unlike chess tactics puzzles procured by human experts, where chess beginners can learn valuable insights, these puzzles are automatically generated and often regarded as having low pedagogical value. These platforms also rely on a heuristic to recommend puzzles to users for practice. Using the user history data over an entire year, a total of 1.5 billion puzzle-solving histories, we learn the pedagogical value of a puzzle and how to automatically choose a set of puzzles to better support chess learners using insights from offline reinforcement learning. We show that using offline policy evaluation, our trained policy has significant impact on beginners with puzzle-solving Elo range of 100--1000, particularly for the group of beginners whose learning growth was stagnant. We also performed a qualitative analysis of the puzzles discovered by our model by collecting annotation ratings from expert chess players. The success of our pipeline shows promise for a future where we can understand the pedagogical values of practice items given general user interaction data.

## 2. 方法论

### 2.1 问题形式化
- 把推荐问题建模为离散动作空间的随机决策过程 $M = \langle S, A, T, r, \gamma \rangle$，其中状态 $s \in \mathbb{R}^k$ 为用户历史的嵌入表示，动作空间为 $N = 441{,}113$ 道谜题。
- 由于动作空间巨大，标准 actor-critic 中对整个动作空间归一化不可行，因此采用**嵌入式策略（embedding-based policy）**。

### 2.2 离线策略学习目标
- 在带 KL 散度约束的 actor-critic 目标基础上，引入优势函数（baseline）得到：

$$
\arg\max_{\theta} \; \mathbb{E}_{s \sim D}\Big[\mathbb{E}_{a \sim \pi_\theta(\cdot|s)}\big[Q^{\pi_\phi}(s,a) - V^{\pi_\psi}(s)\big] - \beta\, D_{\mathrm{KL}}\big(\pi_\theta(\cdot|s) \,\|\, \pi_b(\cdot|s)\big)\Big]
$$

- 由 Nair 等人（2020）的推导，其闭式最优解可投影回参数空间，得到**带权最大似然目标**：

$$
L_{\pi}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{D}\big[-\log \pi_\theta(a|s)\exp\big(\tfrac{1}{\beta}(Q^{\pi_\phi}(s,a)-V^{\pi_\psi}(s))\big)\big]
$$

- 价值网络用 **expectile regression** 训练：

$$
L_V(\psi) = \mathbb{E}_{D}\big[L^{\tau}_2(Q^{\pi_\phi}(s,a)-V^{\pi_\psi}(s))\big]
$$

- 动作价值网络用标准 TD 目标：

$$
L_Q(\phi) = \mathbb{E}_{D}\big[(r(s,a)+\gamma V^{\pi_\psi}(s')-Q^{\pi_\phi}(s,a))^2\big]
$$

### 2.3 模型架构
- 采用**因果 Transformer** 作为策略 $T_{\theta_T}$，输入为拼接的用户特征 $u_t$ 与谜题特征 $p_t$ 的嵌入；每个序列位置同时输出下一题预测 $\pi(a|s)$ 与价值 $V(s)$。
- **用户嵌入器**：两层 MLP $U_{\theta_U}$，输出经 L2 归一化。
- **谜题嵌入器**：MLP + CNN（编码棋盘 FEN 位置）+ 谜题 ID 与首步嵌入 $P_{\theta_P}$。
- **策略参数化**（公式 7）：

$$
\pi_\theta(a|s_t) = \frac{\exp(\lambda\, h_t^{\top} p_a)}{\sum_{a' \in A}\exp(\lambda\, h_t^{\top} p_{a'})}
$$

- 因全空间归一化代价过高，采用**In-Batch 负样本近似**（公式 8）：

$$
\hat{\pi}_\theta(a_{t+1}|s_t) = \frac{\exp(\lambda\, h_t^{\top} p_{t+1})}{\sum_{p_n \in P(B)}\exp(\lambda\, h_t^{\top} p_n)}
$$

### 2.4 奖励函数设计
奖励综合正确率与相对难度：

$$
r(s,a) = \frac{c}{N}\exp\!\big(\alpha \cdot (\mathrm{Elo}_{\text{puzzle}} - \mathrm{Elo}_{\text{user}})\big)
$$

其中 $c/N$ 为正确着法比例，$\alpha=0.002$ 通过内部调参确定，答错时奖励为 0。
