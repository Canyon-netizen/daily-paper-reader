## 摘要
我们考虑由相对阶大于一的未知非线性动力学建模的多智能体系统具有规定瞬态性能的分布式距离编队控制问题。我们引入一个虚拟领导者，其轨迹由指定的智能体子集以规定瞬态行为进行跟踪。无向通信图为树图或最小无穷小刚度图。在后一种情形下，我们进一步考虑质心跟踪目标。在每种情形下，我们设计了分布式且无需模型的控制律，并证明了编队与跟踪误差满足规定漏斗约束，以及闭环信号的有界性。数值仿真验证了所提控制律的有效性。

## Abstract
We consider distributed distance-based formation control with prescribed transient performance for multi-agent systems modeled by unknown nonlinear dynamics of relative degree greater than one. We introduce a virtual leader whose trajectory is tracked with prescribed transient behavior by a designated subset of agents. The undirected communication graph is either a tree graph or a minimally and infinitesimally rigid graph. In the latter case, we further consider the objective of centroid tracking. In each setting, a distributed and model-free control law is developed, and we establish the satisfaction of the prescribed funnel constraints on the formation and tracking errors, and boundedness of all closed-loop signals. Numerical simulations illustrate the effectiveness of the proposed control laws.
