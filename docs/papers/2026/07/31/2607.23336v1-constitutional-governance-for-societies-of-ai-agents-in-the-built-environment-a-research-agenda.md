## 摘要
建成环境正处于用自主人工智能体填充自身的临界点。在改造、运营和出行领域，用于建议、控制与协调的AI系统的部署速度，快于对其集体行为的研究速度。主流的叙事框架将每个智能体视为作用于被动建筑的工具，将治理简化为单一智能体的安全性，这种做法并不充分。更准确地说，一栋建筑、一条街道或一座城市应当被建模为一个由谈判智能体组成的社会：居住者、所有者、运营者、监管者，以及越来越多代表他们行事的AI智能体。它们之间的交互是策略性的，信息是不对称的，而真正重要的结果是作为整体涌现的属性。本文提出了一项针对建成环境宪制多智能体治理的研究议程，围绕三个问题展开：深度不确定性下改造的机制设计，并将公共补贴视为机制组成部分；建筑运营中智能体的物理信息验证；以及冲击下城市基础设施的反脆弱协调。本文借鉴了三十年来的多智能体系统研究，奠定了概念基础，详细推演了一个治理事件，识别出十二个开放性问题，并概述了该议程在数据、计算、学科整合与治理方面的需求。

## Abstract
The built environment is on the cusp of populating itself with autonomous artificial agents. AI systems that advise, control and coordinate are being deployed across retrofit, operation and mobility faster than their collective behaviour is studied. The dominant framing treats each agent as a tool operating on a passive building, governance reduced to single-agent safety, which is inadequate. A building, a street, or a city is more accurately modelled as a society of negotiating agents: occupants, owners, operators, regulators, and the artificial agents increasingly acting on their behalf. Their interactions are strategic, their information asymmetric, and the outcomes that matter are properties of the whole. The paper proposes a research agenda for constitutional multi-agent governance of the built environment, organised around three problems: mechanism design for retrofit under deep uncertainty, treating public subsidy as a mechanism component; physics-informed verification of agents in building operations; and antifragile coordination for urban infrastructure under shocks. Drawing on three decades of multi-agent systems research, the paper sets out conceptual foundations, works one governance event through in detail, identifies twelve open problems, and outlines what the agenda requires in data, computation, disciplinary integration and governance.
