## 摘要

自监督学习（SSL）已成为一种有效的范式，可从大规模无标签全切片图像（WSI）中学习可迁移的表示。然而，现有的自监督学习方法主要学习通用的视觉特征，往往无法显式地捕捉对疾病表征至关重要的病理学特异性形态模式。为了解决这一局限性，我们提出了基于病理学感知原型蒸馏的微型视觉 Transformer（TVT-PAPD）。该自监督病理学表示学习框架将微型视觉 Transformer（TVT）与一种新颖的病理学感知原型蒸馏（PAPD）模块相结合。PAPD 采用一个可学习的病理学原型库来发现并保留具有代表性的组织形态模式，鼓励语义上相似的病理学区域学习到一致且具有判别力的表示。该所提框架在仅使用 90M 参数保持计算效率的同时，增强了病理学感知的特征学习能力。在癌症基因组图谱（TCGA）低级别胶质瘤（LGG）/胶质母细胞瘤（GBM）数据集以及印度病理学脑部（IPD-Brain）数据集上的实验表明，TVT-PAPD 在 LGG-GBM 分类任务中分别取得了 93.02% 和 90.23% 的加权 F1 分数，并在独立的胶质瘤数据集之间展现了强大的跨队列泛化能力。

## Abstract

Self-supervised learning (SSL) has emerged as an effective paradigm for learning transferable repre-sentations from large-scale unlabeled whole slide images (WSIs). However, existing SSL methods primarily learn generic visual features and often fail to explicitly capture pathology-specific mor-phological patterns that are critical for disease characterization. To address this limitation, we propose Tiny Vision Transformer with Pathology-Aware Prototype Distillation (TVT-PAPD). This self-supervised pathology representation learning framework integrates a Tiny Vision Transformer (TVT) with a novel Pathology-Aware Prototype Distillation (PAPD) module. PAPD employs a learn-able pathology prototype bank to discover and preserve representative tissue morphology patterns, encouraging semantically similar pathological regions to learn consistent and discriminative repre-sentations. The proposed framework enhances pathology-aware feature learning while maintaining computational efficiency with 90M parameters. Experiments on the Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) low-grade glioma (LGG)/glioblastoma (GBM) dataset and the Indian Pathology Brain (IPD-Brain) dataset demonstrate that TVT-PAPD achieves weighted F1-scores of 93.02% and 90.23%, respectively, for LGG-GBM classification, while exhibiting strong cross-cohort generalization across independent glioma datasets. Furthermore, the learned prototypes capture meaningful patterns in tissue morphology, improving the interpretability of the representation. These results highlight the effectiveness of pathology-aware prototype distillation for efficient, robust, and interpretable WSI analysis in computational pathology.
